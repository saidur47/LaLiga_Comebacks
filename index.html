La Liga Comebacks: Who Wins After Trailing at Half-Time?

CIS5930 Assignment 2 – Md Saidur Rahman

Real Madrid are famous for comebacks. Is that true in La Liga, and are they really different from other clubs? This dashboard looks at every league match from 1995/96 to now and asks: when a team is losing at half-time, how often does it still win?

1. Who comes back?

Each dot is one club in the selected seasons. X axis: how often the club was behind at half-time. Y axis: how often it still won those matches. Bigger dot = more matches played. Dashed lines are the league average. Clubs with fewer than 5 such matches are hidden. Click a dot to select a club, Shift+click to compare.

2. When did it happen?

Number of matches per season where the club was behind at half-time, coloured by the final result. Drag across the chart to pick a range of seasons (click outside the selection to clear it). The last season is still being played. Play seasons moves a window of seasons forward one season at a time, and all charts follow – watch the clubs move in chart 1.

3. Half-time score vs final result

Rows are the situation at half-time, columns are the final result. Each cell shows how many matches and the percentage of that row. Darker = higher share. Click a cell to highlight those matches in chart 2 and the table; click it again to clear.

4. Match list

The matches behind the current selection, newest first. Scores are from the selected club's point of view.

Data: football-data.co.uk – Spanish La Liga results with half-time scores. 1993/94 and 1994/95 are not used because they have no half-time scores.
